⚾ Strike Zone Cam

Take a picture of a batter, put the strike zone on them, then watch the pitches come in.

Photos from your camera roll, texts or files all work. Pictures stay on this device.

📸 Take their picture

Get the whole person in, from the top of their head to their feet.

🟩 Make the strike zone

The zone is as wide as home plate (17 inches), from just under their knees up to halfway between their belt and shoulders. Drag it to move it, drag a corner to change its size.
👆 Tap where the pitch should go
Press ⚾ Pitch! to throw one. You'll see the pitch come in, and exactly where it crossed the plate.
